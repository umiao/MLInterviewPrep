## §5 Promotion candidates flagged for meta-prep

Per protocol §5, this section lists "patterns spotted in this company that meet the `promotion_criteria.md` threshold (>=3 of 11 P0+P1 companies AND de-companiable wording)."

**Lyra has zero promotion candidates.** Reasons:

1. **No interview-prep patterns exist.** The S1 checklist covers personal-health session prep (sleep / mood / back pain / FMLA paperwork / medication adherence). None of these are de-companiable to a `meta-prep/*` interview-prep node.
2. **No cross-company recurrence possible.** Lyra is the only mental-health-provider row. The other 32 companies in scope are all recruiting companies; their prep prose has zero overlap with therapy session prep. Threshold (>=3 P0+P1 companies) is mathematically unreachable.
3. **Meta-prep substrate is interview-only.** The existing `meta-prep/*` taxonomy (behavioral-clusters / system-design-must-knows / code-pad-best-practices / lc-keyword-checklists / onsite-loop-templates -- per T-P1-803 through T-P1-807) is interview-prep substrate. Adding a `meta-prep/health-session-checklists/*` cluster would be a category error: it would conflate the application's two surfaces (recruiting prep vs. personal-health tracking).

### Anti-promotion note

If a future contributor sees the Lyra checklist and thinks "this is a re-usable session-prep template, let's promote it," the right answer is: **no.** The Lyra checklist is intentionally personal (back / bipolar / FMLA / medication) and the value is in its specificity to the user. De-companying it produces a useless skeleton. Document this here so that B4-promotion (T-P1-821) does not accidentally pick it up.
