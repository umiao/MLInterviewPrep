## TL;DR (the headline finding -- read this first)

Adobe is the **opposite** of the Slack case (B4a-slack). Slack's S3 was a one-time recruiter-screen artifact with almost no reusable content. Adobe's S3 is a **~270 KB structured ML-knowledge corpus** (Day 1-8 + a final-round Stats/RAG set) covering diffusion models, RLHF/DPO, distributed training, RoPE/long-context, inference optimization, transformer internals, multimodal, LoRA, statistics, and RAG. It is the **purest instance of the "duplicated reusable prose" class this protocol exists to fix** -- but the duplication is *cross-surface against the KG*, not cross-company.

Three findings drive the plan:

1. **The KG already canonically covers ~95% of Adobe's technical content.** Adobe shows `kg_refs=0` only because these docs never *linked* to the KG -- not because the knowledge is missing from it. A keyword sweep maps every major Adobe cluster to a substantive existing `framework_nodes` row (5-12 KB descriptions each), and a spot-check confirms the signature claims are present in the target (DDIM/CFG/ControlNet in kg://200; DPO/KL/SFT in kg://229; FSDP/ZeRO in kg://126; YaRN/NTK in kg://201; AWQ/GPTQ in kg://157; CLIP/LLaVA in kg://164; LoRA/QLoRA in kg://154; HyDE/rerank in kg://163). **This is a high-yield, low-risk archive** -- the targets exist and are verifiable.

2. **doc id=19 (125 521 chars, "All-in-One Day 1-8") is a pure rebuilt aggregate** of docs 12-20, produced by `scripts/rebuild_allinone.py`. Its 398 headings are byte-for-byte the concatenation of the Day docs. It carries **zero unique content** -> the single cleanest DELETE candidate (drop with no migration row needed beyond "covered by docs 12-20, themselves migrated").

3. **The interview schedule is already fully internalized.** S1's "Interview Schedule" table (interviewers Yiming Xu / Julia Viladomat, 2026-03-31) and the later onsite panel are already rows in `interview_events` (ids 6, 7, 43, 68, 70-74). Provably no loss on archive.

**Genuinely Adobe-specific content that must be RETAINED (not dropped)** is small and isolated: the JD/role framing (Senior MLE GenAI, Firefly/Acrobat/Express/Experience Platform), the **Firefly Video strategy** note (doc 14 SS5.6), and the "design an Adobe-scale text-to-image system" framing (doc 16 SS7). These go into the SS3 skeleton.

**Two clusters have NO existing KG target** and are surfaced as SS5 promotion candidates, NOT dropped: **video generation / DiT / Sora** (doc 14 SS5) and **GANs** (doc 17 SSF).

Net recommendation: **content-swap** the 10 S3 docs to a thin drawer-link skeleton + DELETE the aggregate (id=19); **defer** the two SS5 clusters to promotion before their rows are dropped. This is the highest-yield archive in the B4a wave so far.
